Workspaces as cards

Home is a grid of workspace cards; the last card adds one. Inside a workspace, sessions, files and people are cards too. The home sidebar stays as it is today; the code window and file tree only appear once you open a session.

1 · Home

Each card is a workspace: a colour cover with its initial, name, which space it belongs to, a line of description, counts, and who is in it. A green pill means sessions are open right now. The dashed card adds a workspace. A switch at the top right narrows to Personal or an org.

5 · Where agents work

Three places an agent can be added, each with a say over sessions. Account level (your Settings › Agents, or the org's Agents page on heyquilt.com): the agent is global, in every workspace, with a default for sessions. Workspace level: added to one workspace, with its own sessions setting. Session level: added to one session by hand, exactly as today. The most specific setting wins.

Plan unchanged, layout changed

  • Home is the workspace grid. The sidebar stays as today (Sessions menu, Home, Settings). No Workspaces list in the sidebar; the cards are the navigation. A Personal / org switch filters the grid.
  • Add workspace is a card that turns into the form in place.
  • Inside a workspace: Sessions cards, Files tiles, People & agents cards on one page, with an All files view for the whole library. No tabs.
  • Loose sessions are a small strip under the grid, with Join and New session where they are today.
  • Agents have a reach and a sessions default. Account-level (global) agents are in every workspace; workspace agents in one. Each says whether it joins every session or only when invited; a workspace can override a global agent's default; a session owner can keep an agent out of one session. The most specific setting wins.
  • Data model, API, access and phases stay as in the spec.
